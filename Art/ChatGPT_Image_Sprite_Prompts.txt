1. GLOBAL STYLE GUIDE — READ BEFORE GENERATING ANY SPRITE
================================================================================

GAME CONTEXT:
Adventure Legacy is an isometric 2.5D tactics RPG in the style of Final
Fantasy Tactics Advance (FFTA). The game features a rogue-like legacy system
where characters die and leave behind legacies for the next generation. The
world is medieval-fantasy with magic, guild factions, and a living world that
evolves as the player progresses. The tone is serious but not grimdark —
think FFTA meets Tactics Ogre with a touch of roguelike grit.

VISUAL STYLE:
- PIXEL ART, high-detail "HD pixel" style (think Octopath Traveler sprites
  or modern FFTA fan art at higher resolution)
- Characters should feel like they belong on an isometric tile grid
- Rich color with strong silhouette readability — every job must be instantly
  distinguishable from every other job at small scale
- Slightly stylized proportions: ~3-head-tall chibi-esque (like FFTA sprites),
  NOT realistic proportions. Heads are large relative to body. Hands and feet
  are simplified but expressive.
- Clean outlines: 1-2px dark outline on all sprites for grid readability
- Characters should convey personality through POSE and GEAR, not facial
  detail (faces are small at game scale)

COLOR PHILOSOPHY:
- Each job has a SIGNATURE COLOR (listed per job below). This color should
  dominate 40-60% of the character's palette.
- Secondary colors should complement, not compete. Use desaturated neutrals
  (leather browns, steel greys, off-whites) as supporting tones.
- Avoid neon/oversaturated colors. The palette should feel grounded and
  painterly, like watercolor over ink — even in pixel art.
- Team tinting will be applied in-engine (blue for player, red for enemy),
  so the base sprite should work as a neutral/NPC version. Design around
  the job color, not the team color.

LIGHTING:
- Top-down directional light from the upper-left (northwest), consistent
  with the isometric camera angle.
- Simple two-tone shading: base color + one shadow tone + one highlight tone.
  No complex gradients. This is pixel art, not painted illustration.

ANIMATION CONSIDERATION:
- For initial generation, create STATIC IDLE POSES only.
- Design the pose so it can be broken into animatable parts later
  (head, torso, arms, legs, weapon as separate conceptual layers).
- Facing: Generate the SOUTH-FACING (toward camera) version. The game
  uses 4 cardinal directions; we will mirror/redraw the other 3 later.

MOOD / REFERENCE BOARD:
- Final Fantasy Tactics Advance (GBA) — proportions, silhouette language
- Fell Seal: Arbiter's Mark — color richness, detail level
- Octopath Traveler — HD pixel art quality bar
- Tactics Ogre: Reborn — tone, grounded fantasy feel
- Fire Emblem (GBA era) — battle sprite expressiveness

DO NOT:
- Do not use anime face detail (eyes, nose, mouth should be minimal pixels)
- Do not make characters look like modern/sci-fi — strictly medieval fantasy
- Do not add background or environment — sprites on TRANSPARENT BACKGROUND
- Do not use gradients or soft shading — hard pixel edges only
- Do not generate real-world weapons (guns, etc.) — swords, staffs, bows only

================================================================================
2. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
================================================================================

FORMAT:        PNG with transparent background
RESOLUTION:    64x64 pixels per frame (can generate at 256x256 and downscale)
               If generating at higher res for detail, maintain the pixel art
               aesthetic — do NOT smooth/anti-alias edges during downscale.
CANVAS:        Single character centered on canvas, feet near bottom edge,
               ~4-8px padding on all sides for animation breathing room
COLOR DEPTH:   Limited palette per character — aim for 12-16 colors max per
               sprite (forces the pixel art discipline)
OUTLINE:       1-2px dark outline (near-black, not pure #000000 — use a
               darkened version of the dominant color for more life)

WHAT TO GENERATE PER JOB (Phase 1 — idle sprites):
  1. IDLE POSE — south-facing, neutral/ready stance, weapon visible
  2. (Future) Attack frame, Cast frame, Hit frame, Death frame, Walk cycle

FOR THIS DOCUMENT: Generate idle poses only. One image per job.

4. SPRITE SHEET LAYOUT NOTES (for future animation phases)
================================================================================

Once idle sprites are approved, we will expand to full sprite sheets:

FRAMES PER DIRECTION (4 directions: S, W, N, E):
  - Idle:        2 frames (subtle breathing/sway loop)
  - Walk:        4 frames (tile-to-tile movement)
  - Attack:      3 frames (windup, strike, follow-through)
  - Cast:        3 frames (charge, release, cooldown) — mages only
  - Hit/Hurt:    2 frames (impact, stagger)
  - Death/KO:    3 frames (stagger, fall, prone)
  - Special:     2-3 frames (job-specific — backstab, steal, etc.)

TOTAL PER CHARACTER: ~68 frames (17 per direction × 4 directions)
SHEET FORMAT:        Horizontal strip per animation, all 4 directions stacked
FRAME SIZE:          64×64 each, consistent across all jobs

For now: IDLE SOUTH-FACING ONLY. One image per job. 14 total images.

6. JOB TREE VISUAL REFERENCE (for understanding progression relationships)
================================================================================

This shows how jobs connect. When generating sprites, the VISUAL EVOLUTION
should be apparent — advanced jobs should look like believable upgrades of
their prerequisites.

  STARTER TIER          ADVANCED TIER         CROSS-TREE TIER
  ─────────────         ──────────────        ────────────────
  Squire ──────────┬──→ Soldier
                   └──→ Knight ─────────┬──→ PALADIN
                                        │
  White Mage ──────┬──→ Bishop          │
                   │                    ├──→ (Knight + WM)
                   └──→ Time Mage ──┬───┘
                                    └──→ SAGE (WM + Time Mage)

  Rogue ───────────┬──→ Assassin ───┬──→ SHADOW (Assassin + Ninja)
                   └──→ Ninja ──────┘

  Dark Mage ────────── (no advanced branches yet — future expansion)
  Archer ───────────── (no advanced branches yet — future expansion)

VISUAL EVOLUTION GUIDELINES:
  - Squire → Soldier: More armor, bigger sword, military bearing
  - Squire → Knight: Full plate, big shield, slower/heavier feel
  - Rogue → Assassin: Darker, more lethal, single blade focus
  - Rogue → Ninja: More dynamic, red accents, thrown weapons
  - White Mage → Bishop: More ornate, golden, authoritative
  - White Mage → Time Mage: Mysterious, cosmic, temporal motifs
  - Knight + White Mage → Paladin: Holy warrior, light radiates, peak noble
  - Assassin + Ninja → Shadow: Pure darkness, featureless, terrifying
  - White Mage + Time Mage → Sage: Ultimate mage, most ornate, cosmic power

Art Director Notes:

When reviewing generated sprites, I will assess against three criteria:
  1. STYLE CONSISTENCY — Does it match the FFTA HD pixel art brief?
  2. SILHOUETTE READABILITY — Can you tell the job at 64x64 without zooming?
  3. EVOLUTIONARY COHERENCE — Does the advanced job visually "descend" from
     its prerequisite? Can you see the Squire in the Knight?

If ChatGPT Image generates at a higher resolution, we will handle downscaling
in-house. The priority is getting the CHARACTER DESIGN right — proportions,
color, silhouette, gear, pose.

Loop in the UI Developer for any HUD/portrait crop specs once idle sprites
are approved. Loop in the Programming Lead to confirm the sprite import
pipeline in Godot (AnimatedSprite2D vs Sprite3D billboard — TBD based on
the 2.5D rendering approach).
