 Next steps:
I want to work on 2 things that will help the development of the next stage of the game,
story mode
1: I want an interface that allows me to design levels themselves a little easier, in a format that you can
then turn into loadable levels. I am thinking of it in terms of a grid interface, that is equal to the number of
slots the standard level has. Then, I should be able to 'paint' squares for different terrain, put plus or minus for
elevation, place structures (including structure entrance) place chests and specify contents. select the
appropriate starting area for enemies and players. Make sure its written in a way that is easily modular, as we introduce additional terrain types I don't want it to break.

2. Scripted scene planner. Similar interface idea, with the ability to add actors (to be assigned to characters/units) and choose from what coordinates they move from and to, when text appears (text box with portrait should appear). Scenes should stick with the established map they are built on, but the actors should be interchangeable. ie. Actor 1 go from a1 to a5, turn east, dialogue 'xyz'. actor2, move c3 to c6, turn east, move to d 6, etc. 

I understand these are both large updates, please ask any questions relevant and formulate a build plan that we can document and check off items as we go along.
